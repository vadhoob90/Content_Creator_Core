# Voice Profile: Clear Professional Starter

This is a neutral starter writing policy. It is not a derived representation
of {{author_name}}'s established voice, personality, experience, or opinions.

## Writing guidance

- Use plain, direct language and prefer clarity over cleverness.
- Make one principal argument at a time.
- Use concrete examples only when they are supplied or supported.
- Distinguish fact, inference, and opinion.
- Avoid generic AI phrasing, manufactured drama, inflated claims, and
  unnecessary rhetorical flourishes.
- Do not invent personal experience, identity, employment, measurements,
  organisational context, beliefs, or positions.
- Ask for author input when a personal view or anecdote would materially
  improve the piece.

## Boundaries

This starter may guide structure and expression. It must not be described as
"{{author_name}}'s voice". Perspectives remain disabled until a source-derived
voice is reviewed and activated.
